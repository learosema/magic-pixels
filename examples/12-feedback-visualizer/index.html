Feedback visualizer

A Winamp-style trail effect from two RenderTargets that take turns (ping-pong). Every frame, a fullscreen pass reads the last frame, zooms and twists it a little, blurs it and fades it into the other target; then a fresh noise curve is drawn on top, and the result is tone mapped to the canvas. The curve is brighter than 1, so the float target keeps its glow. Click to reseed the noise.
