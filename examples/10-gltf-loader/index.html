glTF loader

loadGltf(url) fetches a Khronos sample model and turns it into a scene: nodes, meshes, PBR materials, textures, cameras and lights, framed from its computeBoundingSphere(). Drag to turn it. Drop your own .glb (or a .gltf together with its .bin and images) anywhere on the page to load it from memory through the fetch option.
